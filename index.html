About

Memes with a studio behind them.

Ticker — $VETOR Chain — TON vetor-studio.ru ↗

Vetor is a creative meme token on the TON blockchain, backed by Vetor Design Studio. It is a meme coin where holding actually unlocks something real: discounted design work, sticker packs and a share of the trading tax.

Every trade carries a 2% tax. 75% of that tax is paid out to holders, so the community is rewarded simply for staying in. On top of that, creator fees feed buyback and burn cycles that reduce circulating supply over time.

Holders get a 20% discount on Vetor Design Studio services, exclusive Telegram stickers and ongoing holder perks. Launched on Stonks, traded on TON.
